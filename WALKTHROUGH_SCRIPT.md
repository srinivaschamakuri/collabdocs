# Suggested 3–5 minute walkthrough

0:00–0:30 — Introduce the scoped product and chosen stack.  
0:30–1:40 — Sign in, create a document, format text, rename, save, refresh, reopen.  
1:40–2:15 — Import a `.txt` or `.md` file.  
2:15–3:10 — Share from account A to registered account B; show B's read-only access.  
3:10–4:00 — Explain Supabase RLS, JSONB, the share RPC, and tests.  
4:00–4:40 — Explain tradeoffs, next steps, and actual AI use.
